The problem

Care coordination falls on the family, with no system to hold it

A single diagnosis can mean referrals across cardiology, physical therapy, and a family doctor — each with its own paperwork, timeline, and office to call back. Today that tracking lives in someone's memory, a notebook, or a pile of discharge papers. When it slips, the cost isn't a missed email — it's a delayed appointment or a referral nobody followed up on.

This project started from watching my own family navigate exactly this kind of coordination burden during my grandfather's cancer care — tracking appointments, referrals, and follow-up steps across visits.

How it helps

From instruction to tracked task, automatically

A small, focused tool that does one job well: hold the next step, and make sure it's visible.

Turn instructions into tasks

Paste a clinician's instructions and CareLoop Zero drafts concrete follow-up tasks — with dates only when one is actually stated, never invented.

See what's overdue, at a glance

One dashboard shows what's pending, what's overdue, and what's done — filterable, so nothing needs to be held in your head.

Built for the messy reality of care

No account setup, no complexity. One page, built to be open on a phone in a waiting room.
